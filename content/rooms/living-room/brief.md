The living room should be the main arrival moment. I want it to feel **colorful, safe, personal, and music-forward**, with enough energy that I feel excited to be home and enough comfort that it works for everyday life with kids.

- Large studio monitors as part of the room's identity
- Credenza or storage piece as an anchor
- Bold wall color
- Gallery wall with photos, art, posters, and kid art
- Comfortable seating
- Pattern through rug, pillows, textiles, or art
- Warm layered lighting
- Closed storage for quick reset
