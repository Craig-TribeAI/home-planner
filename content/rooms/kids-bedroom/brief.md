The kids' bedroom should feel like a **sleep-friendly bunkroom clubhouse**, even without bunk beds. I want it to feel magical, cozy, safe, and a little adventurous, while still being calm enough for sleep.

- Two low beds or floor beds
- Glow-in-the-dark stars
- Cozy clubhouse feeling
- Fun color, not pastel
- Books, lamps, rugs, and layered bedding
- Personal zones for each kid
- Keep most active play elsewhere
