The entryway is the first impression — it should feel **warm, personal, and intentional** the moment you walk in, and stay practical for a family coming and going.

- Warm welcome: art, a mirror, or a personal moment right at the door
- Hidden storage for shoes, bags, and coats
- A clear surface for keys and the daily drop
- Warm layered lighting, not a single overhead
- Durable, easy-to-clean materials underfoot
