The master bedroom should feel like a **calm retreat** — restorative, minimal, warm, and grounded, more like a quiet boutique retreat than a decorated bedroom. The feeling should be soft, airy, sandstone-like, and peaceful.

- Low-profile bed
- Minimal furniture
- Sandstone, oat, cream, warm white, taupe, and pale clay colors
- Warm wood, linen, cotton, and woven textures
- Large soft rug
- Simple nightstands or floating nightstands
- Gentle lamps and layered lighting
- A few plants
- Minimal, quiet art
- Possibly a slim table near the window
