## Overall home

I want the townhouse to feel **intentional, warm, creative, and deeply personal**. The house should have a clear point of view without feeling over-designed. I'm drawn to color, music, art, plants, texture, and family life, but I also want enough simplicity and storage that the home feels calm and easy to live in.

- A compact home with a strong sense of identity
- Color and pattern used intentionally
- Warm, natural materials
- Personal art, photos, books, music, and plants
- Kid-friendly spaces that still feel considered
- Clear surfaces and hidden storage where possible
